## 生平简介

> Sarah Goher is an Egyptian-American writer and producer, born and bred in the South Bronx. An alumna of NYU Tisch and the Nightingale Bamford School, Sarah worked on "Cairo 678" (2010), "Clash" (2016), "Amira" (2021) and Marvel Studios' "Moon Knight" (2022). "Happy Birthday" (2025) marks her directorial debut.
